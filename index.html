About Our School

Our Journey

Founded in 2000, Modern School has been at the forefront of educational excellence, serving thousands of students and families in our community.

We believe in fostering a learning environment that combines academic rigor with personal development, preparing students not just for exams, but for life.

Our experienced faculty and state-of-the-art facilities ensure every student receives the best education possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get Answers to Common Questions

What are the admission requirements?

▼

Admission is based on merit, entrance examination, and an interview. We look for motivated students who are ready to engage in our comprehensive curriculum. Please visit our admissions office for detailed information about the process.

What is your school timings?

▼

Our school operates from 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM on weekdays. We also offer extended programs for sports and cultural activities until 5:00 PM. Admission is on a rolling basis throughout the year.

Do you provide transportation?

▼

Yes, we provide safe and reliable school bus services covering major areas of the city. Transportation fees are separate and can be customized based on your route requirements.

What extracurricular activities are available?

▼

We offer a wide range of extracurricular activities including sports, music, dance, art, debate, robotics, and more. Students are encouraged to participate and develop their talents beyond academics.

What is your scholarship policy?

▼

We believe in providing quality education to all deserving students. We offer merit-based and need-based scholarships. Interested students can apply during the admission process or contact our scholarship committee.
